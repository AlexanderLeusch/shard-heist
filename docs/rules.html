Game rules

These documents describe what the game does, as implemented in the code. When a rule changes, update the document, the code and the tests together, then run npm run docs to rebuild the printable version.

The game has two rooms that are played one after the other. Until the group connects them, each room is its own page.

The whole game in one paragraph

You were the detective on the Ashford necklace case. Now you are inmate 4471, convicted of stealing it. Room 1: you have ten minutes in a prison washroom to find the code for a hatch in the floor. If you get out (Ending A), a note shows that the guard wanted you out, and a car is waiting at the end of the tunnel. Room 2: the police chase begins. You drive a getaway vehicle through Lisbon's Baixa, on the highway and across the countryside, collecting star shards while up to four police cars and a helicopter hunt you.

One night, one look

  • Time: both rooms are set on the same night: room 1 around 02:15, room 2 from about 02:30.
  • Design: both rooms share one design system: the same background, glass panels, fonts, buttons and cards. Each room keeps its own accent colour: orange in the prison, cyan on the run. Hale's radio messages in room 2 are orange because he comes from room 1.
  • A test (tests/page.test.js) checks that the shared design tokens and the story links between the rooms stay in place.

How the two rooms will connect (planned, not built)

  • Ending A of room 1 shows a button that opens room 2.
  • Ending B of room 1 stays in room 1 (try again).
  • Open question for the group: does room 2 then start in free ride, or straight in the story mode The Necklace Job?

Room 1 · Plot Twist: Cell Block D

This document describes the rules in escape-room/src/rules.js. Values marked (open) are not decided yet.

Goal

Open the hatch in the floor of the toilet stall and climb out before the clock reaches zero.

Story

  1. Briefing. You were the detective on the Ashford necklace case. Tonight you are inmate 4471, convicted of stealing the necklace: someone framed you.
  2. Officer Hale walks you to washroom 2, locks the door and says: "Ten minutes. Not a second more." He waits outside.
  3. Inmates whisper about a hatch in the floor of this washroom, locked with a four-digit code that is hidden somewhere in the room.

The clock only starts when the player presses Start the clock after the briefing.

Clues and the code

ClueHow to find itText
MirrorTurn on the hot tap first. Then the steam shows letters someone traced. Without steam you only see your face."UNSINKABLE?"
GraffitiRead the scratches on the stall wall"She was unsinkable. She sailed only once. The year opens the floor."
Newspaper scrapPry the tile off with the spoon, then read the paper behind it"...the 'unsinkable' liner went down on her first voyage. 15 April 19▒2..."
  • The code is 1912, the year the Titanic sank. It is the same in every game (open: should it change between games?).
  • Players who do not know the history can still solve it: "sailed only once" gives the missing digit 1.
  • Every clue you read is kept in your clue notes (3 at most). Reading a clue again does not count twice.

The lock

  • Four wheels, each showing 0–9. Up after 9 is 0, down after 0 is 9.
  • You can only turn the wheels and try the code while kneeling at the hatch.
  • Try the code:

- Right code: the hatch opens, Ending A. - Wrong code: "Clunk. The lock holds." Hale hears the noise, and you lose 30 seconds. The number of wrong codes is counted.

Endings

EndingWhenWhat happens
A · Out belowThe right code before time runs outYou climb down the ladder. A note is taped to it: "Took you long enough. A car is waiting at the end of the tunnel. — H." The guard wanted you out. That is the plot twist that leads to room 2.
B · Back to the cellThe clock reaches 0:00 (after the extra minute, if the door was jammed)The door bangs open and Hale and two officers take you back to cell 4471.

The earlier decision: jamming the door early gives one more minute at the very end. That can turn Ending B into Ending A.

After an ending, every action is refused except Play again / Try again, which starts a new game from the briefing.

Saving

  • The game is saved in the browser after every accepted action and every few seconds while the clock runs.
  • After a reload, the player can Continue (with the time that was left) or start a New game, which asks for confirmation first.
  • A saved game is checked field by field when loaded. Anything that is not a real save is ignored, and a new game starts.

Room 2 · Shard Heist

This document describes the rules in src/core.js, and the pickups and police in src/game.js.

Where room 2 starts

Room 2 begins where room 1 ends. You (inmate 4471, the framed detective) have climbed out of the tunnel under Cell Block D. It is about 02:30 at night. In a lock-up garage, Officer Hale's getaway vehicle is waiting. Every police car in town is now hunting the escaped inmate.

Officer Hale is a secret ally. He believes you were framed, but he cannot prove it. In the story mode he helps over the radio; his messages are shown in orange, the colour of room 1. Police dispatch messages are red.

Two ways to play

  • Free ride: pick a map and a getaway vehicle, then escape.
  • *Story mode, The Necklace Job:* three acts on the three maps, in order, with a story and three endings.

  • A shard is picked up within 3.8 m; a repair kit within 3.6 m, and only when you have damage.
  • Lisbon has traffic lights, other cars and three yellow trams. The trams only run on their tracks, a stone track bed with two rails set into the road: a clockwise loop through Rua da Conceição, Rua da Prata, the north side of the Praça do Comércio and Rua Áurea. As on real Lisbon streets, cars and the player can drive over the tracks. The trams stop at red lights and behind other vehicles. On the highway traffic drives both ways. On country roads there is oncoming traffic, and driving off the road cuts your top speed to 55 %.
  • Each map has an edge you cannot cross. Near it the screen turns red and a "turn back" arrow appears.

The city map: Lisbon's Baixa

The Baixa is the centre of Lisbon, rebuilt as a street grid after the 1755 earthquake. The map is a simplified version of it: the blocks are square, and the grid has 7 streets each way. The real Baixa has a few more, so some are left out. The real Baixa is also flat; the hills on this map are a game decision.

DirectionStreets, in their real order
West to eastRua do Crucifixo, Rua Áurea, Rua dos Sapateiros, Rua Augusta, Rua dos Correeiros, Rua da Prata, Rua dos Fanqueiros
North to southRua de Santa Justa, Rua da Vitória, Rua de São Nicolau, Rua da Conceição, Rua do Comércio, Praça do Comércio (north side), Avenida Infante Dom Henrique
  • In free ride, the panel at the top left shows the street or square you are on. In story mode it shows the act instead.
  • You start on Rua Augusta, facing south towards the Arco da Rua Augusta.
  • Squares: the Rossio and the Praça da Figueira in the north, the Praça do Comércio by the river in the south. The statue on the Praça do Comércio stands in the middle of the road, so traffic passes on both sides.
  • Landmarks you can see but not reach: the river Tejo, the 25 de Abril bridge, Cristo Rei and the castle hill. The Santa Justa lift stands on the map, and you can crash into it.
  • Parks: there are three, which the real Baixa does not have. The story needs them for the sealed letter.
  • Houses: closed blocks of Pombaline houses, wall to wall, as the Baixa was rebuilt after 1755: a ground floor plus 3 to 5 floors (OpenStreetMap gives most buildings 5 levels), arched stone ground floors with old shop fronts, the tallest windows and the balconies on the first floor, limewash colours, and tile roofs with dormers. Tiled fronts are the exception, as they are in the real Baixa.
  • Real buildings in their place: the National Theatre and Rossio station north of the Rossio, the yellow arcades and corner towers of the Praça do Comércio, and the Sé, seen at the end of Rua da Conceição. Their position is simplified, and they are modelled freely, not measured.
  • It is always about 02:30 at night. The clock on the arch shows it.

Hills. All three maps have hills, up to about 20 % steep. The Arco, the river front and the two ends of the highway are level. Uphill your top speed falls by up to 32 % and gravity pulls you back. Downhill your top speed rises by up to 22 % and you coast on. The truck feels slopes most, the motorcycle least. Brakes and rolling friction hold a stopped vehicle in place.

Helicopter. After 60 seconds on the run (or sooner with the fifth star), a police helicopter joins the chase for the rest of the run and hunts you with its searchlight. Its searchlight follows you at 94 % of the police speed. While you are in the light (within 9 m of its centre), the police drive 6 % faster, and in the city they see you from 170 m instead of 110 m. It cannot bust you or damage you. At full speed on a clear road you can stay ahead of the light.

Radio

Seven stations, or off: Reggae, Techno, Hardstyle, Schlager, Sing-Along Pop, Classical and Heavy Metal. Choose in the menu, or with R or the radio button while driving. The music plays while you drive, and is silent in the menu and in pause.

All of it is composed and synthesised by the game, with no music files. The tunes are generated by the game. The classical station plays Bach and Beethoven, whose works are in the public domain.

Saved settings

The last map and vehicle, the best times, the radio station and the graphics setting are saved in the browser. Every saved value is checked when loaded; invalid values fall back to Lisbon, Car, radio off and the defaults.
